# H1: TIER-FALL. Power gates kill; significance gates promote

*Synthesist H1, 2026-10-05. Emphasis: the decision and gating framework. No repository file was edited. The power simulations are in the scratchpad only: `hybrid/h1_power.py` → `h1_power.json`, `hybrid/h1_oc.py` → `h1_oc.json`.*

**Thesis.** Build E's light FLUSH model. Judge it only on B's fall arena, using one pre-registered ladder with four outcomes: **SHIP, SHIP-WITH-BADGE, HOLD, KILL**. The ladder obeys one rule that resolves the E-versus-B conflict:

> **A gate may kill the programme only if (a) it found significant harm, or (b) it is a futility gate whose minimum detectable effect (MDE), computed before any real outcome is seen, is no larger than the pre-registered minimum important effect. Failing to show a benefit never kills. At worst it holds.**

B's battery fails closed for the product: the status quo stays primary until the model earns its place. Under this rule it no longer fails closed for the programme, which was the false-kill risk raised by R4, R5 and R7. E's union-pass kill rule, which R1, R2 and R6 criticised, is replaced by an intersection–union conjunction with α = 0.05 per binding gate.

## 2. The hybrid design (complete, compact)

### 2.1 Estimand (E §3.1 = B §3.2)
F(C, t) is expected fall encounters for a standard walk: one median-skill observer, traveling, 60 min, 2 km, 15 Oct, 08:00. It is evaluated as the **stencil mean of exp(h_fall) over the standard-walk footprint centred in the covert**, which fixes V11's change of support. κ converts to flushes per hour, is display-only, and is fitted from the owner's logs and NH regional rates.

### 2.2 Data and years
- EBD + SED for ME/NH/VT, filtered per Johnston et al. 2021 (E/B): complete; Stationary/Traveling; 5–300 min; ≤ 5 km; ≤ 10 observers; one per `GROUP IDENTIFIER`.
- **Habitat-model rows: 2020–2025 only (V1).** The ingest CR asserts that every feature of every row is within ±2 years and **refuses the run otherwise** (mirrors `filter_by_year_gap`).
- 2010–2019 checklists are used only for out-of-fold observer skill, the clock-only panel, and pre-trend leads.
- Border strips are **dropped** for now (R6, PA-0017/0023: LCMS, LANDFIRE and OPERA have no coverage in QC/NB). Their reinstatement is a separate CR.

### 2.3 Arenas (the core power fix)
- **Super-block cross-fitting (R2's V2 fix).** 5 folds of 25 km super-blocks. Fold k's model trains on the other folds, with a 2.5 km buffer only at the edges of fold-k super-blocks. My Monte Carlo puts the training share kept at **≈ 92%** (uniform points), against 24–31% under a flat buffer around HH. Every checklist receives an out-of-fold (OOF) score.
- **HH**: checklists whose **start point** lies in a CNN validation block, with the split taken from `regions.block_split(block_ids, block_assignments)` and **never** from `block_assignments.csv` alone (V6). The CNN is compared only here. HH checklists are scored by the fold model that excluded their super-block.
- **OOF-all**: every fall checklist's OOF score. Used for any gate that does not involve the CNN.
- **Temporal arm** (C via R4): train on ≤ 2023 and test on 2024–25. Advisory.

### 2.4 Features (V11 fixes)
- Legacy block: the `diagnose_gbm_baseline.py` design, with **categorical codes converted to class fractions per radius** before stencil averaging, and fixed multi-radius columns (90/250/600 m). There is no radius chosen per row.
- `road_dist` is excluded from H and kept as a diagnostic (B).
- Clock: Hansen v1.12 `lossyear` plus LCMS 2025-11 change and land use, fetched by the **CR-0034 route** (V5), with no fused HF437 (V9). Outputs are age-class shares at 90/250/600 m, diversity, and a slow-loss flag.
- The 2026 freshness overlay (DIST-ANN 2023–24 now; DIST-ALERT later, through LP DAAC) is **product-only**, not a model feature.

### 2.5 Model (E §3.3, simplified)
P(y = 1) = 1 − exp(−exp[g(e) + 1_spring·βᵀo + H(Z̄)]).
- g is the event-only GAM (E/B): no location-derived variable and no species count.
- H is LightGBM with a **row-wise** cloglog objective. Its Hessian is non-negative but ≈ Λ/2 on detection rows (R3), so `min_sum_hessian_in_leaf` is set explicitly and a **Hessian finite-difference test** is committed (V4).
- B's pixel-scale MIL/LSE objective is an optional stage 2, **with the Gauss–Newton clamp H·s_m²**. It is admitted only through G-ADMIT (§3.2).
- The ensemble is 5 folds × 2 seeds.

### 2.6 Detectability (E's triad, with B's use-shift truth)
- H (season term set to fall), H_fall (fitted only on Sep–Dec, unshrunk) and the visual-mode head (only if ≥ 500 coded detections per mode, B) are compared.
- The ranking rule is E's: h_fall = H if all pairs reach the calibrated threshold, otherwise a stack. **The threshold is injection-calibrated** against four planted truths: no-tilt; spring-audibility tilt; **fall use-shift** (B's and R5's critique of E); and a preferential-sampling variant. Each gets ≥ 50 draws.
- If the calibration cannot separate no-tilt from tilt at ≥ 90%, the rule defaults to **always stack** (E).
- The triad is a *decision*, not a hypothesis test, and carries no α (§3.5).

### 2.7 Preferential sampling and leakage battery
Gate status is set in §3.2:
- same-day case-crossover CC (D);
- first-visit CC (D/E);
- the label-permutation band with **100 refits** (D; V8; PA-0021(c));
- drop-targeters (B);
- off-trail importance-weighted TkL (E);
- **clock-only** stacked event-study panel with leads (C via R2/R6; V3);
- forest-only partial ρ against controls (C), as a diagnostic.

### 2.8 Product
1. **H250 v0** (A/D form, built from the `tsd` rasters regenerated by CR-0035; R4's cheap v0). It carries access classes A1–A4/X, a daily list of 4 Thompson-sampled coverts plus 1 uniformly random covert, and is frozen and hashed before any test.
2. **Covert card v1** (E/B). It shows F with an 80% interval, peak window, freshness, access, walk-in distance and top-3 reasons, with these badges:
   - extrapolated;
   - audible-not-flushable;
   - PS-fragile;
   - **support badge "data-driven / ecology-driven"**, with support measured geographically (A, as amended by R1);
   - the **map-level tier banner** (§3.4).
3. Rule-out layer (E).
4. The owner's hunts are logged as eBird-style complete checklists with a `hunt` protocol (B). They are held out in season 1.

## 3. The decision and gating framework

### 3.1 Seven principles

| # | Principle | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Fall decides.** Every binding comparison is computed on Sep–Dec checklists. | B6 | A drumming map cannot pass on spring audibility. No identification assumption is needed (R2). |
| P2 | **The MDE comes first.** Before any real label is scored, T0 measures each statistic's sampling SD on the *real* checklist geometry with injected labels. A futility gate is binding only if MDE80 ≤ MIE. | New (V7; R1, R4, R5) | This prevents B's false-kill failure mode. |
| P3 | **Power gates kill; significance gates promote.** KILL needs significant harm or a powered futility result. Not reaching significance on a benefit can cap the tier, never kill. | New | This is the asymmetry the owner can live with. |
| P4 | **A conjunction is an intersection–union test.** All binding promote gates must pass, each at one-sided α = 0.05, with no Holm adjustment. | R2 (Berger's IUT) | An IUT already holds level α. Holm on a conjunction only costs power (R2 finding 4). |
| P5 | **The procedure is the unit under test.** T0 runs the full tier logic on planted truths and cheaters. A cheater must reach SHIP in ≤ 5% of ≥ 50 draws. | B's cheater battery + PA-0021(a)/(c) | This shows the ladder can fail the way it should. |
| P6 | **Fail closed for the product, not for the programme.** Until a tier ≥ SHIP-WITH-BADGE, H250 stays primary. | Compromise of B and E | The owner always hunts with something. A wrong ship costs more than a late ship (R2). |
| P7 | **A hold is bounded.** There is one pre-registered re-look, and its inconclusive default is pre-registered. | New | Prevents an endless "collect more data" loop, which eBird cannot satisfy anyway (+17% per season). |

Why these are the binding gates. G-SIG stops the "no signal" and effort-artefact maps (cheater C4, via the band). G-NI stops "worse than what the owner already has". Both are fall-decided, so they also stop C3, a drumming map, whose fall AUC suffers. G-CC stops C1 only when it is powered. C2, return to known spots, cannot be stopped by any powered fall gate on these data volumes. **That is an accepted residual** (§3.8), and it is badged through O-FV.

### 3.3 The minimum-detectable-effect computation (T0, before any real outcome)

**Procedure (committed in CR-0036, run once the T-1 counts exist):**
1. Take the real filtered 2020–25 checklists: locations, effort, observer-days, 25 km folds and HH flags.
2. Plant synthetic labels from truths with a known map gap: CNN-like versus model-like scores at ΔAUC ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.025, 0.04}; no-signal; and the cheaters C1–C4.
3. For each gate statistic, compute its estimate and a **block bootstrap over 3 km blocks within 25 km super-blocks**. Use ≥ 50 draws per truth (≥ 100 where a p99 is quoted; PA-0021(c)).
4. Compute MDE80 = (z₀.₉₅ + z₀.₈₀) × SD = 2.49 × SD, per gate and arena.
5. Apply the mechanical rule: **a kill-capable gate is binding iff MDE80 ≤ its MIE; otherwise it is demoted to O for this look, and the demotion is written into every artifact of the run** (PA-0021(d) escape).
6. Run the full tier logic (§3.4) on every planted truth to produce the operating-characteristics table, which must meet the targets in §3.7.
7. Freeze: the register, MIEs, margins, α schedule, thresholds and the T0 output hash are committed **before** `eval_tiers.py` sees real labels.

|---|---|---|
| ΔAUC_strat, model vs effort-only (G-SIG futility) | 0.02 | Below this, the map barely ranks places at all |
| ΔAUC_strat vs CNN, non-inferiority margin δ_NI | 0.01 | The most loss tolerated in exchange for the better estimand and product |
| ΔAUC_strat vs CNN, superiority | reported against 0.025 | About half the prior gap of 0.05 (E/B priors) |

**Reading these numbers:**
1. **The TkL₅ gate cannot decide anything on HH.** Its MDE80 of about 0.58× exceeds the expected gain of about 0.44×. Power at the expected effect is Φ(0.44/0.235 − 1.645) ≈ 0.59. Under a six-gate Holm correction (first step at α = 0.0083) it falls to about 0.30. This is the quantitative core of the V7/B objection. Using TkL₅ as a kill gate on HH, as B and E both do, would kill a working model about 40–70% of the time.
2. **AUC is about 2.3× more efficient** for the same underlying improvement (MDE/expected ≈ 0.58 for AUC, against ≈ 1.32 for TkL₅). AUC therefore leads the fixed sequence, and TkL₅ is a "bonus claim" (reached in about 50% of working-model runs).
3. **Restricting to Oct–Nov raises the MDE by about 20–40%** relative to Sep–Dec (TkL₅ 0.71 against 0.58; AUC 0.033 against 0.024). R5 noted that September is pre-shuffle and December is winter cover. The hybrid **decides on Sep–Dec and reports Oct–Nov** as O.
4. **The signal gate on OOF-all is well powered.** Its futility power at true zero signal is Φ(0.02/0.0066 − 1.645) ≈ 0.92, so it may kill.
5. **Fall CC on HH is hopeless.** It has tens to low hundreds of pairs, with MDE ≥ 0.10 against an expected gain of 0.025. CC is moved to all-season OOF-all pairs, where it is binding only if T-1 counts ≥ about 1,800 pairs.
6. **The next season barely helps.** Look B shrinks MDEs by only about 7% (√(7/6)). This is why HOLD must be bounded (P7).

### 3.4 Tier logic (pre-registered pseudo-code in `eval_tiers.py`)

```
if any I-gate fails:                         -> STOP (fix; no tier)
if G-SIG.futile (and G-SIG binding):          -> KILL   (no fall habitat signal)
if G-NI.harm vs CNN or vs H250:               -> KILL as replacement (significantly worse)
if not G-SIG.pass:                            -> HOLD
if not G-NI.pass (vs both comparators):       -> HOLD   (cannot rule out "worse")
if G-CC is binding and fails:                 -> HOLD
if G-SUP step 1 (ΔAUC > 0) passes:            -> SHIP
else:                                         -> SHIP-WITH-BADGE
```

The tier banner on the map shows:
- **SHIP**: "beats the old map on fall checklists", listing the sequence depth reached (AUC / TkL₂₀ / TkL₅).
- **SHIP-WITH-BADGE**: "not worse than the old map in fall; better estimand; superiority unproven".

Cards carry O-badges regardless of tier.

**What each tier means for the owner:**

| Tier | Primary layer | Fitted map | Programme |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHIP | Fitted FLUSH layer | Primary | Continue: optional blocks via G-ADMIT, season-2 W(s) and κ |
| SHIP-WITH-BADGE | Fitted FLUSH layer, with a banner | Primary | Continue; re-test superiority at Look B |
| HOLD | H250 (status quo) | "Unproven" overlay; daily list still from H250 | One re-look (Look B); no new model features until then |
| KILL | H250 | Withdrawn (KILL-harm: overlay removed; KILL-futile: archived) | Checklist programme stops. H250, access classes, ledger and field arm continue. Re-opening needs a new CR with a new data source (e.g. agency flush data, GMNF coordinates). |

### 3.5 Multiplicity handling (one family per question)

| Family | Procedure | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Binding promote gates (G-SIG, G-NI, G-CC if binding) | **Intersection–union**: each at one-sided α = 0.05, no adjustment | All must pass, so the FWER of a wrong promotion is ≤ α (R2). Holm here only manufactures false kills. |
| Superiority claims (G-SUP) | **Fixed sequence** AUC → TkL₂₀ → TkL₅, each at the look's α | Ordered by power, so the powered claim is never spent on the unpowered one. |
| Optional feature blocks (G-ADMIT) | **Holm** across the blocks proposed in the same round (k ≤ 4) | These are parallel "any of" claims, where Holm is the right tool. |
| Two looks (A, B) | **Lan–DeMets O'Brien–Fleming spending** at information fraction t₁ = 6/7: Look A z ≥ 1.82 (α₁ = 0.034); Look B z ≥ 1.74 (nominal 0.041); overall one-sided 0.05 | Computed in the scratchpad with scipy. Applies to G-SUP and G-NI. Futility and harm use their fixed rules at both looks, and are non-binding for α. |
| O-gates and badges | Unadjusted, labelled OBS, never promote | PA-0021(d) |
| D-gates (triad, forecast) | No α. Calibrated classification accuracy ≥ 90% on planted truths | These are choices between two options, not claims |

My operating-characteristics simulation (§3.7) used α = 0.05 at a single look. With α₁ = 0.034 at Look A, the SHIP rates for small gains fall slightly. A working model is unaffected (its z is about 4).

### 3.6 What "inconclusive" triggers: the HOLD protocol

1. **Entry.** G-SIG passes, but G-NI is inconclusive, or a binding G-CC fails; or G-SIG is neither pass nor futile.
2. **During HOLD:**
   - H250 stays primary, with the randomised slot unchanged.
   - The fitted map appears only as an overlay labelled "unproven" (P6).
   - No features are added. G-ADMIT is frozen, so that tuning cannot be fitted to the re-look.
3. **Look B trigger.** The first EBD release that contains December 2026 (timing **unverified**; expected Jan–Feb 2027). Re-run `eval_tiers.py` unchanged on 2020–26, at Look B's boundaries.
4. **Look B extra input.** The season-1 field arm and the S0 rescoring are added as O-evidence only. They are too weak to bind (≥ 2× detectable).
5. **Look B outcome.** The same tier logic applies, with one change: **an inconclusive result at Look B is pre-registered as RETIRE-TO-OVERLAY.** Concretely:
   - H250 stays primary;
   - the fitted map remains as an optional overlay;
   - work on the checklist arm stops.

   This is a KILL for development, not for the artifact.
6. **Why no third look.** Each further season adds about 1/7 of the information, so the MDE falls about 7% per year. Bringing the TkL₅ MDE down to the expected gain (0.58 → 0.44) needs about 1.7× the information, which is about 4–5 more seasons. Waiting is not a strategy. A new data source is, and that is a new CR.

### 3.7 Operating characteristics (simulated; T0 must reproduce these on real geometry)

P(tier | truth). HH has 830 and OOF-all has 4,150 fall detections; 200 replicates per truth.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Worse than the CNN (ΔAUC ≈ −0.013) | **0** | 0.03 | 0.615 | 0.355 | 0 |
| No habitat signal | 0 | 0 | 0 | **1.0** | 0 |

**Pre-registered acceptance targets for T0** (PA-0021(a)/(c)):

| Requirement | Target |
|---|---|
| P(SHIP) for a working model | ≥ 0.8 |
| P(SHIP or SWB) when worse by ≥ δ_NI | ≤ 0.05 |
| P(SHIP) for each cheater C1–C4 | ≤ 0.05 |
| P(KILL) when the model is equal to or better than the CNN | ≤ 0.10 |

Simulated against those targets:
- a working model reaches SHIP in 0.995 of runs;
- a worse model is promoted in 0.03;
- a model equal to the CNN is killed in 0.055.

The cheater cases need real geometry and are measured in T0. If a target is missed on real geometry, the thresholds are **not** tuned toward a pass. The failing gate is demoted to O and the tier is capped at SHIP-WITH-BADGE. **This rule is pre-registered.**

### 3.8 Errors accepted, and errors refused

| Error | Accepted? | Rate (simulated) | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promoting a worse map to primary | **Refused** | ≤ 0.03 | This is the costliest error for a hunter (R2) |
| Shipping a map equal to the CNN, with a badge | Accepted | 0.16 | It has the better estimand, intervals, access classes and fall ranking. The badge tells the truth. |
| Killing a model equal to the CNN | Accepted at ≤ 0.10 | 0.055 | Equal is not worth the programme cost |
| Killing a working model | **Refused** | ≈ 0 | This is B's failure mode, removed by P3 |

## 7. What H1 gives up

**Relative to pure E:**
- **Speed.** The fitted map arrives about 2 weeks later than E's stated date, because the MDE and operating-characteristics freeze must precede scoring.
- **Lenient survival.** E's union-pass rule kept a model alive on a spring-driven CC win. H1 will HOLD it. Acceptable: H1's promote errors are bounded at ≤ 0.05.
- **TkL₅ as a headline gate.** It becomes a sequence-tail claim reached about 50% of the time even when the model works. Acceptable: it was never powered on HH.
- **The season-contrast term as a default member of the triad.** It now has to pass G-ADMIT. Acceptable, given R2's cross-sectional identification critique.

**Relative to pure B:**
- **Fail-closed for the programme.** An underpowered gate no longer kills. Acceptable: the product still fails closed, which is the error R2 cared about.
- **Holm across the battery.** It is replaced by an intersection–union test. Acceptable: the IUT holds the same level with more power.
- **The pixel-scale MIL default.** It is now optional stage 2. Acceptable: V4 makes it fragile, and R3's support concern is met by stencil-mean prediction.
- **Several gates downgraded to advisory.** First-visit, panel, cross-play and drop-targeters are now OBS. Acceptable: none is powered at fall volumes, and an unpowered binding gate is a coin flip.
- **The C2 residual.** No gate stops a return-to-known-spots map. This is stated, not hidden.

**Relative to both:**
- One more CR (the tier harness) and two pre-registered looks, about 3 owner-days of process. That is the cost of a decision procedure the owner can trust and cannot argue with afterwards.
