# H4: FLUSH-EB "Two Arenas": E's model and product, B's fail-closed discipline, decided where the data can actually decide

*Synthesist H4, 2026-10-05. Emphasis: the evaluation arena and data reality. No repository file was edited. Simulation code: `scratchpad/hybrid/h4_sim.py`, output `h4_sim.out`.*

**Thesis.** E and B disagree mainly about *where* decisions are taken. B takes every decision on fall checklists in HH, and that sample is too small. E takes its decisions in a held-out set that its own 2.5 km buffer drains, and against a CNN whose access to the data it never matched. This hybrid splits evaluation into two arenas, each used only for the questions it can answer:

1. **Arena P (parity).** HH checklists only, scored by a model trained on *exactly the CNN's label access*: every non-HH checklist, with no buffer. This is the only arena where the CNN can be scored fairly, and it is used only for the CNN comparison (gate G1).
2. **Arena O (out-of-fold).** Every 2020+ checklist, scored out-of-fold on 24 km super-blocks, with buffers only at super-block edges. Each fit keeps about 76% of all checklists, against about 26% under E's flat 2.5 km buffer. Arena O carries about 5× HH's fall detections, so it decides everything that does not involve the CNN: the fall ranking function, feature-block admission, the permutation band and the clock.

The fall-only question that B wanted to gate on is therefore answered in arena O, where it has power: the minimum detectable effect (MDE) on the fall top-5% lift ratio is about 1.15×, against about 1.42× in HH. In arena P, fall acts as a **non-inferiority veto** rather than a superiority requirement.

## 3. The arena design (core of this proposal)

### 3.1 Objects

- **Block grid.** `regions.BLOCK_SIZE_M = 3000`, `BLOCK_ORIGIN_5070 = (0, 0)`, `to_5070`, `block_ids`.
- **HH.** Every filtered complete checklist, 2020–2025, whose **start point's** block is `"val"` under `regions.block_split(block_ids(*to_5070(lon, lat)), data.block_assignments)`. That is the CNN's validation set, including the unlisted blocks (V6).
- **Super-blocks.** 24 km squares, each exactly 8 × 8 blocks, on the same origin. Every 3 km block therefore lies in exactly one super-block, and HH blocks never straddle folds. 24 km replaces E's 25 km so the grids nest; it is still checked against the residual variogram.
- **Folds.** Super-blocks are visited in `order_key("sb:" + id)` order and each is assigned greedily to the fold with the fewest **fall checklists so far**. This balances effort, never outcomes, is deterministic and uses no CLI seed (PA-0001/0025 style).

### 3.2 The three arms

| Arm | Training set | Scored on | Used for | Buffer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P (parity)** | Every 2020+ checklist **not** in HH, by start point. Hyperparameters chosen by super-block CV *inside* non-HH. | HH | **G1 only** (the CNN comparison) | **0**, which is the CNN's own rule: its positives are eBird start points in training blocks, and its 64-px window reaches about 0.96 km into HH blocks, just as our footprints do |
| **O (out-of-fold)** | All folds except k, minus training checklists whose truncated footprint (R_j ≤ 2.4 km) + 0.5 km reaches a fold-k super-block. Nested inner CV for hyperparameters. | Every checklist in fold k (HH included) | G2–G4, triad, clock, panel cross-fitting, injection, everything model-internal | Edges only |
| **T (temporal)** | Arm O restricted to 2020–2022 | 2024–2025 | Forecast back-test (V10) | As O |

**Why P is fair to the CNN, and its bias.** P and the CNN have the same label access: training-block eBird detections, membership by start point, nothing from HH blocks. Two leaks favour only the CNN: checkpoint selection on validation blocks (`train.py --select-by rank`), and envelope weights computed from all sightings. These are accepted. They bias G1 *toward a false kill, never toward a false ship*, which is the safer error. Arm O scored on HH is reported beside P as the stricter, extrapolation-only number. If P passes and O-on-HH has the opposite sign, G1 becomes INCONCLUSIVE.

**PA-0029.** Arm P draws only from training blocks, so it is compliant by construction. Arm O is a separate split. Its fits carry fold provenance in their metadata and are never compared under the CNN's split except when scored out-of-fold. The shipped full-data fit is made only after every decision is frozen.

### 3.3 Retention (simulated; replaced by a measured, gated count on day 1)

Simulation: `h4_sim.py`. It uses the real hash rules (md5 and blake2b with `SPLIT_SEED=42`, listed blocks filled to 20% of records). The geography is synthetic and is an **assumption**: 600k checklists in a 360 × 390 km domain, 45% at hotspots, 40% around towns, 15% uniform; 35% stationary; traveling distances log-normal with median 1.5 km, capped at 5 km; footprint R = min(2σ, 2.4 km) with σ = √(0.15² + (0.35D)²).

| Scheme | Share of non-HH (or other-fold) checklists kept | Share of **all** checklists in each fit | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| E/C/D: flat 2.5 km around HH blocks | **0.32** (fall detections kept 0.29) | 0.26 | Matches R2/R3/R4 (24–31%). |
| B: footprint + 0.5 km around HH blocks | 0.60 | 0.48 | Traveling ≥ 3 km kept: **0.25**. It biases training toward short checklists. |
| **P: parity** | **1.00** | 0.80 | CNN-equal access. |
| **O: 24 km out-of-fold, flat 2.5 km at edges** | 0.92 (min fold 0.90) | 0.74 | |
| **O: 24 km out-of-fold, footprint + 0.5 km at edges** | **0.95 (min 0.95)** | **0.76** | Chosen. |

Fold loads (fall detections) came out at 425–434 per fold. HH holds 19.9% of checklists and 20.1% of detections.

**Gate G0-R (exact, pre-registered).** On real EBD, every arm-O fit must hold ≥ 65% of all 2020+ checklists *and* ≥ 65% of all fall detections. HH's traveling share and distance terciles must lie within 5 pp of the full set (B's representativeness check). If either fails, the CR stops before any fit is made.

### 3.4 V6, quantified

Building HH from `block_assignments.csv` alone (listed, occupied val blocks only) **keeps 64% of HH checklists, raises the HH detection rate 1.55×** (0.034 vs 0.022) and raises the hotspot share from 0.45 to 0.56 (simulated). That is selection on the outcome.

**Test (exact, CR-0037, written before approval):** the arena's HH set equals an independent recomputation through `block_split`, and contains at least one unlisted block. A constructed wrong build (listed-only) must fail it (PA-0021(a)).

### 3.5 The panel test as a clock-only check (V3)

- **Units.** eBird locations with ≥ 8 checklists at −5…−1 years and ≥ 8 at +1…+12 years around a stand-replacing loss covering ≥ 10% of the 300 m disc. Loss comes from LCMS Fast Loss ∪ LANDFIRE disturbance, with LCMS Land Use forest→forest. Cut cohorts 2013–2022, checklists from EBD 2010–2025.
- **Prediction.** Δη̂ comes from a **clock-only** sub-model (age-class shares, guild, elevation) fitted in arm O. A site in fold k is predicted by the fold-k model, which is trained without that site's super-block. This is the cross-fit, at no extra cost.
- **Estimation.** A stacked event study (C) with site fixed effects, region × year effects and leads −3…−1 as a placebo. The output is the slope of the observed change on Δη̂, and the **identified age range**, which is expected to be about 1–12 years. Nothing beyond it is claimed.
- **Status: OBS.** It can demote the clock's age curve on the card to "prior". It never kills the programme. Power is counted at T0.

### 4.2 MDE by arena

MDE is for 80% power with a one-sided α of 0.05. TkL ratio values are block-bootstrap SEs from the simulation, rescaled by √(detections) to the real counts above. CC and AUC values are analytic (Hanley–McNeil and binomial, design effect 1.5).

| Statistic | HH fall (P) | HH all-season (P) | All checklists, fall (O) | Expected effect (E/B priors) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top-5% lift ratio, model/CNN | **1.42×** | **1.24×** | 1.15× (model vs model only) | ≈ 1.25–1.33× |
| Effort-stratified ΔAUC | 0.057 | **0.021** | 0.025 | 0.04–0.05 |
| Same-day CC (pairs: HH fall ~10², HH all ~10³, O all ~5×10³; **unverified**) | ≈ 0.12 | 0.03–0.04 | 0.012–0.018 | 0.03–0.05 |

**What this means.**
- Fall superiority over the CNN on HH has about **0.5 power at best** on the lift ratio and much less on CC. Gating on it (B) would stop a working programme about half the time.
- All-season ΔAUC on HH is well powered (≈ 0.95 at a true effect of 0.04). The all-season lift ratio has about 0.6–0.9 power.
- Fall questions *internal* to the model (H vs H_fall, clock admission) reach an MDE of about 1.15× in arena O.

## 5. Model (complete, kept lean)

1. **Ingest.** EBD + SED, ME/NH/VT plus border strips, 2010–2025 (Johnston et al. 2021 filters). Rows flagged for HH, fold, first visit, mode label, same-day pairs and panel units. Habitat rows: **2020–2025**.
2. **Footprint.** A 16-point stencil with σ = √(150² + (0.35·D)²) m, truncated at min(2σ, 2.4 km) (one rule for the model and the buffer).
3. **Features at each stencil point, averaged.**
   - One-hot fractions for evt (grouped), sclass, fdist, nlcd.
   - Continuous means: evh, evc, ch, cc, tcc, TreeMap, disturbance-masked `mch_*`, gradient, slope.
   - Clock: age-class shares 0–4/5–10/11–20/21–40 yr at 90/250/600 m, plus age diversity.
   - **Footprint σ itself as a feature.**
   - Every feature year must be within ±2 of the checklist year. This is an exact assertion in the ingest CR: the tolerance is asserted, never silently resolved.
4. **Fit.**
   - *g* is a GAM offset. H is a LightGBM row-wise cloglog with `init_score` = *g*, alternated 3–5 rounds.
   - A unit test checks the gradient **and Hessian** against finite differences and asserts Hessian ≥ 0 (V4). It sets `min_sum_hessian_in_leaf`.
   - H_fall is the same pipeline on the gate window only, unshrunk.
   - The visual-mode head is fitted only if ≥ 500 coded detections exist per mode.
5. **Prediction.**
   - Feature year **2025**, so every layer is within tolerance: LANDFIRE 2024/25, `tcc` 2023 (gap 2) until the 2024–25 extension lands, `nlcd` 2025. The clock is aged to 2026 with LCMS 2025 plus LANDFIRE 2024.
   - Prediction is at **stationary support** (σ = 150 m stencil), then aggregated to coverts.
   - The card says: "landscape as of 2025, clock aged to 2026; cuts after 2025 not mapped".
6. **Ranking function h_fall** (gate G3): H if the injection-calibrated agreement rule says H and H_fall agree in arena O. Otherwise, and whenever the rule is underpowered, a stack of H and H_fall with weights fitted on arena-O fall deviance.

## 6. Gates: binding vs advisory, and what "inconclusive" does

Every row is labelled GATE or OBS (PA-0021(d)). Every non-exact threshold is calibrated on ≥ 50 fair and ≥ 50 broken draws on the real EBD geometry (T2). If the two distributions overlap at the real sample size, the row is **demoted to OBS automatically** (PA-0021(c)). This is how "underpowered" is kept separate from "failed".

| ID | Arena | Statistic | Type | PASS | FAIL | INCONCLUSIVE / demoted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 | all | HH = `block_split` recomputation; year gap ≤ 2 for every feature; PA-0049(b) registration on every layer read; retention G0-R; fold provenance | GATE (exact) | Run proceeds | Fix and re-run; nothing is fitted | — |
| G1 | **P on HH** | Co-primaries, **all-season**, month-stratified: (i) effort-stratified ΔAUC; (ii) log top-5% lift ratio of h_fall vs the CNN. Holm at family α = 0.05 (one-sided), so G1 passes if either survives Holm. **Fall veto:** fall (gate-window) ΔAUC and log lift ratio must not fall below −δ, with δ set by T2 (fair "equal fall skill" vs broken "spring-audibility map"; indicative δ ≈ 0.2 log-ratio given SE ≈ 0.14) | **GATE (binding)** | Fitted map drives 4 of 5 daily-list slots | **Stop the 2026 checklist programme** if both co-primary point estimates ≤ 0, **or** the fall veto fires (a drumming map is not shipped even if it wins all-season). ## 9. What the hybrid gives up

**Relative to pure E:**
- About two weeks of calendar time, spent on arena code, T2 calibration and two training arms.
- The season-contrast β as a decision input (it is now a diagnostic).
- The forecast index for future seasons, unless O4 passes.
- AlphaEarth and DIST-ALERT freshness in 2026.
- E's lenient AND rule, which would have shipped more often.

These are acceptable because E's faster path rested on a drained training set (V2), an unfair CNN comparison and a leaking back-test (V10). Its extra "ships" would have been unverified ones.

**Relative to pure B:**
- B's pixel-scale MIL objective (its Hessian is broken, V4).
- The rule that *every* gate is decided on fall HH.
- Holm across six gates.
- The cheater battery and cross-play as kill gates (they are OBS here).

These are acceptable because, at the verified fall volume, those gates have power of about 0.5 or less. They would stop a working programme for lack of data, not because the model is wrong. B's protective intent survives in three places:
- fall decides the *ranking function* (in arena O, where it has power);
- a calibrated fall veto blocks a drumming map from shipping on an all-season win;
- the permutation band stays binding.

**Relative to both:** the hybrid does not claim fall superiority over today's map in 2026. It claims a map that is all-season better than the CNN at equal data access, not worse in fall, built from a fall-informed ranking function, and that leaves the field arm to measure the rest.
