## 0. Thesis in one paragraph

The hybrid is ranked by expected value, not by rigour or by elegance. The owner's best map this October is the one he can have this week. That map is **H250**: a zero-fit covert layer built from the `tsd` rasters already on disk, with no new export (R4). H250 is the **floor** of the product. The fitted fall encounter map (E's core) replaces the floor on the phone only after it passes a **fail-closed gate against H250**. Two features of the gate make it both strict and powered:
- **It is decided on fall checklists**, as B requires.
- **It is powered.** H250 is zero-fit, so it can be scored on *every* checklist. The fitted map is scored out-of-fold with 25 km super-block cross-fitting (R2). The decisive fall comparison therefore uses about 5× the detections of the HH-only arena, which removes V7's power problem for the decision the owner actually faces.

The underpowered comparisons (against the CNN, fall case-crossover, first visit) still run. They are advisory or badge rules and never kill the programme.

A gate can end in three ways:
- **pass:** the fitted map ships;
- **inconclusive:** H250 stays primary, the fitted map is shown as a shadow layer, and the decision is repeated with 2026 data;
- **fail:** the habitat programme stops, decided by an equivalence bound and not by a missed significance.

Where birders never walk, each covert card says "ecology-driven", and its rank is pulled toward the ecological floor.

**Honest delivery:** the owner hunts H250 from about **9 October**. The fitted map lands around **23–30 November** at best-likely, if it passes. It is therefore mainly a **2027** product. §8 explains why that is still the highest-EV plan.

### 2.2 Model (2026 path)

**Rows.**
- Complete EBD checklists, 2020–2025, ME/NH/VT. Filters follow Johnston et al. (2021): Stationary or Traveling; 5–300 min; ≤ 5 km; ≤ 10 observers; one checklist per group.
- No border strips. Stencil points with nodata are dropped from the mean. A row with more than 25% of its stencil points nodata is refused (PA-0017).

**Footprint.**
- σ_j = √(150² + (0.35·dist_j)²), truncated at 2σ ≤ 2.4 km.
- 16 stencil points (E).

**Features.**
- Fixed-radius disc means and SDs for continuous layers.
- Class fractions for `evt` (crosswalked to guild), `evc`, `evh`, `sclass`, `fdist` and `nlcd`.
- Clock shares at 90/250/600 m from `tsd` (age classes 0–4, 5–10, 11–20, 21–30, > 30).
- `mch_*` (static, flagged as such).
- Every feature is read at the checklist's year, and every row is asserted to be within tolerance (V1).
- `road_dist` is excluded from H (B) and used only for walk-in distance and the near-road badge.

**Fit.**
- g is a penalised GAM, alternated with H for 3–5 rounds and passed in as `init_score`.
- H is the all-season model.
- H_fall is fitted independently on Sep–Dec rows only.
- Both are trained over 5 folds of 25 km super-blocks × 2 seeds.

**Detectability decision.**
- Compute ρ = Spearman(H, H_fall) over out-of-fold forest coverts.
- If T2 separates the planted truths at > 90% of ≥ 50 draws:
  - ρ ≥ τ\* and H's fall deviance ≤ H_fall's → h_fall = H;
  - otherwise h_fall = a stack of H and H_fall, with weights fitted by nested fall-fold deviance, and an "audible-not-flushable?" badge where the two disagree by more than one quintile.
- If T2 does not separate the truths, always stack.

**Prediction.**
- 2025 features, the clock aged to 2026, and the 0.5 km standard-walk footprint centred on each covert.

### 2.3 Geographic support and the ecological fallback (A + R1)

**Support.** For covert c:

  n_eff(c) = Σ_j Σ_m w_jm · dur_j · K₂₃₀(s_jm − c)

- The sum runs over all filtered 2020–25 checklists, every season.
- w_jm are the stencil weights and K₂₃₀ is the prediction kernel.
- When scoring a checklist, its own observer-day is left out.
- **Support is label-free.** It counts effort, never detections, so computing it on all checklists, including HH, leaks no label (PA-0029 concerns label-bearing signal). The CR states this argument and tests it: a unit test shows that permuting y leaves π unchanged.

**Weight.** w(c) = n_eff / (n_eff + n₀).

**Displayed score.** S(c) = w · pct(h_fall(c)) + (1 − w) · pct(E(c)).
- pct is the percentile among forest coverts in the state.
- E(c) is the ecological floor: H250 in 2026, and A's guild-prior map v1 from 2027 (its own CR).

**Badges.** Data-driven for w ≥ 0.7, ecology-driven for w < 0.3, mixed otherwise.

**Why geographic, not covariate space (R1).** A logging-road checklist through a young cut has the same footprint covariates as the off-trail cut beside it. Covariate support would call the off-trail cut "well supported". Geographic support decays at about 230 m from where people actually walked.

**Choosing n₀.**
- n₀ is chosen in the T0 injection grid from {0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8} checklist-hours, before any real result.
- The planted truth has trail-biased visits and off-trail density, and n₀ is the value that minimises rank error on **unvisited** simulated cells (B's scoring).
- n₀ is **never** tuned on HH or on any decision subset (R2, R3, R6).

**What the blend can and cannot claim.**
- It is a product rule, not a validated gain.
- Off-trail truth in 2026 comes only from S0 and the field arm, which detects only effects of ≥ 2× (A).
- A binding non-inferiority gate (G4 in §3) ensures the blend does not damage ranking where data exist.

### 3.2 Minimum detectable effect (MDE), one-sided α = 0.05, power 0.8

The computation is in this file's working; the formulas are stated so a reviewer can redo them.
- **TkL₅:** SE(log ratio of two lifts) ≈ √(1/O) × 1.75 (block over-dispersion, assumed) × 1.18 (paired overlap, r ≈ 0.3).
- **AUC:** SE(paired ΔAUC) ≈ √(AUC(1−AUC)/n₊) × √(2(1−r)) × 1.3, with r ≈ 0.6.

| Metric | Arena | MDE | Power at the expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall TkL₅ ratio | HH (O ≈ 130) | **1.57×** | 0.41 at a 1.29× gap (E's prior midpoints 2.0 vs 1.55) |
| Fall TkL₅ ratio | Out-of-fold (O ≈ 600) | **1.23×** | 0.92 |
| Fall effort-stratified ΔAUC | HH (n₊ ≈ 900) | **0.044** | 0.81 at +0.045; 0.52 at +0.03 |
| Fall effort-stratified ΔAUC | Out-of-fold (n₊ ≈ 4,500) | **0.020** | ≈ 1.0 at +0.045; 0.98 at +0.03 |

These are planning numbers. The binding MDE is recomputed in CR-0037 from the T0 injection grid and a block bootstrap on real rows **before** any gate is read. If the real MDE exceeds the expected effect, the gate is demoted to OBS (PA-0021(c): "if the distributions overlap, no gate exists").

**Conclusion.** B's design, with every gate fall-decided and computed on HH, kills a working programme about 60% of the time on TkL₅ alone. The same fall decision taken against H250 on out-of-fold data is powered.

### 3.3 Gates: binding versus advisory

All rows are pre-registered in CR-0037 and labelled GATE or OBS (PA-0021(d)). Each threshold is calibrated on ≥ 50 fair and ≥ 50 broken pipelines (PA-0021(c)). In this agent-only workflow, a different agent builds the broken pipelines (PA-0021(a)).

| ID | Row | Data | Label | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Leakage, all-season | Out-of-fold CC (all 2020–25 same-day pairs; thousands expected) | **GATE** | Real CC > the 95th percentile of the permutation band (100 refits) |
| G2 | Leakage, fall | Out-of-fold fall AUC vs the permutation band | **GATE** | > the band's 95th percentile |
| G3 | Ship: superiority | Out-of-fold **fall** effort-stratified AUC, h_fall − H250 | **GATE** | One-sided 95% lower bound > m_AUC, where m_AUC is the injection-calibrated null 95th percentile |
| G4 | Ship: non-inferiority | Out-of-fold **fall TkL₅** ratio h_fall / H250; and blended S vs raw h_fall on fall AUC | **GATE** | Lower 95% bound of the ratio > 0.90; blend loses no more than 0.005 AUC |
| O1 | CNN vs fitted map | HH fall AUC, TkL₅, CC | OBS | Retires the CNN from research if the fitted map wins; never kills |
| O2 | Drumming check | Triad (§2.2) | Decision (not a kill) | Sets h_fall |
| O3 | Preferential sampling | First-visit CC share | OBS → badge | < 50% of the gain survives → "PS-fragile" |
| O4 | Targeters, control species, off-trail weighted TkL, low-support subset | Out-of-fold | OBS | Reported with n |

**Multiplicity.** G1–G4 form one conjunction that must all pass, which is an intersection–union test, so the family-wise false-ship rate is ≤ 0.05 without Holm (R2). OBS rows make no claim, so they need no correction.

**Outcomes.**
- **PASS:** G1–G4 all pass. The fitted map becomes primary on the phone; H250 remains the ecological floor inside the blend.
- **FAIL:** the checklist habitat programme stops for 2026 if either:
  - G1 or G2 lies inside the band (effort manufactures "habitat"); or
  - the **upper** 95% bound of the G3 gain is < +0.01 AUC (equivalence: the fitted map is shown to be no better than H250).

  Restarting it needs a new CR with a stated reason, such as a new data source.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else.
  - H250 stays primary.
  - The fitted map ships as a **shadow layer**, a "model agrees" badge on H250 coverts that are also in the fitted top 20%.
  - The randomised slot draws half the time from the fitted top 30%.
  - The gates are re-run in spring 2027 on 2020–26 data.

**Error budget (planning).**

| Error | Probability | How |
|---|---|---|
| False ship (a no-better map displaces H250) | ≤ 5% | The intersection–union conjunction at α = 0.05 |
| False kill when the true gain is +0.03 AUC | < 1% | The equivalence bound needs the upper bound < +0.01 |
| Inconclusive when the true gain is +0.03 | ≈ 2–10% | |
| Inconclusive when the true gain is +0.015 | ≈ 50% | This is accepted: a gain that small is not worth a phone swap in mid-season |

The errors I accept:
- an "inconclusive" that costs a season of the better map on the phone, which is bounded because the shadow layer and random slot keep collecting evidence;
- no in-season test of the off-trail blend.

## 7. What the hybrid gives up

**Relative to pure E:**
- **Its schedule claims.** E's "fitted map in week 3" is replaced by a plan that says late November, or 2027. That is not a loss of value, only of optimism (R4, R7).
- **The season-contrast term β and E's three-way triad.** Only H vs H_fall decides. Reason: R2 showed β's identification claim is wrong, and B showed the use-shift confound.
- **AlphaEarth and lidar.** Gone (V9; R1 rated their hunter value low).
- **A union-pass kill rule.** E's loose AND-kill could ship a spring-driven win. The hybrid is stricter on what reaches the phone.
- **The 2 km standard walk.** Coverts are scored at the 0.5 km footprint.

**Relative to pure B:**
- **"Every gate on fall HH, Holm-adjusted."** Only the product decision is fall-decided and binding, and it is decided out-of-fold against H250, not on HH against the CNN. The HH/CNN comparison becomes advisory. Reason: §3.2 shows B's arena kills a working programme about 60% of the time on TkL₅.
- **B's pixel-scale MIL** is deferred to optional CR-h. **Cross-play with calibrated m\*** is dropped. **The cross-fitted panel** moves to 2027 and becomes clock-only.

**Relative to A:** covariate-space support is replaced by geographic support; the structural core is deferred to the 2027 floor; lidar is dropped.

**Why this is acceptable.** Each item given up is either:
- untestable this season (β, the panel, the off-trail blend);
- unbuildable this season (MIL, clock fusion, AlphaEarth); or
- an answer to a research question rather than the owner's question (CNN retirement, cross-play).

The owner's question is "which layer do I hunt?". The hybrid answers it with a powered, fall-decided, fail-closed test, and with the floor already in his pocket.
