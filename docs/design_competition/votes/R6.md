## Flaws shared by all five, missed in every round
1. **The registration route contradicts CR-0034 / PA-0049(a). Severity: MAJOR.** A §3.2, B §3.1, C §3.3, D §3.3 and E §3.2 all say "EE export ... on the template lattice with explicit `crsTransform`". That makes Earth Engine resample onto the rotated template grid. This is exactly the route CR-0034 removed. The approved route is a native-lattice request followed by an exact local warp. The gates they name (`grid_mismatch`, the offset probe) check the declared grid. BUG-0094 shows that a check on the declared grid cannot catch this defect. Only A names a content check (`check_layer_registration.py`), and none names PA-0049(b)'s `road_dist` reference. Fix: route every new clock, OPERA or AlphaEarth layer through the CR-0034 functions (`year_native_grid` / `build_raster` / `warp_to_grid`), and sequence it after CR-0035.
2. **Year matching. Severity: MAJOR.** Every design fits on 2016+ checklists (B and A on 2016-2025; C, D and E on "2016+ for fitting") with the legacy 15 layers "for the checklist's own year". For 2016-2019, LANDFIRE has no vintage within tolerance 2, so `filter_by_year_gap` would refuse. Getting past that needs a reviewed change of YEAR_MIN or of the tolerance, and no design proposes either. For the 2026 product season, TCC (latest 2023) is 3 years off, which is also outside tolerance. The issue is worst in the panel tests of B5, C X2, D route 2 and E X2. These use cut years 2005-2019 and checklists from 2010, and need F̂(x_pre) at years where the legacy layers, and AlphaEarth before 2017, do not exist. Either the model check runs on a clock-only F, which none of them says, or it reads post-cut vintages as "pre", which makes the Δ partly identically zero. A's T5 is the exception: it fits the age curve from clock bins with location fixed effects and needs no legacy layer.
3. **Clock source domains (PA-0020/0032). Severity: MEDIUM-MAJOR.** HF437 covers Maine only. In the fused clock ("earliest event confirmed by ≥1 source", or a median plus an agreement count), Maine stands therefore get an extra detection source, so age and agreement shift by state. A, C, D and E also add 10 km border strips into Quebec and New Brunswick. LCMS, LANDFIRE and OPERA are absent there, so "never disturbed" would be written as a legitimate value where the source does not cover. That breaches PA-0017/PA-0023: such cells must be nodata. No design addresses either point.
4. **Permutation band from 20 refits, read at its 95th percentile (B, C, D, E). Severity: MAJOR under PA-0021(c).** The rule requires at least 50 draws. E cites PA-0021(c) for its triad (50 draws) but uses 20 for the band. The fix is cheap: 50-100 refits on the 200k subsample.

## Single most serious unaddressed flaw unique to each design
- **A: MAJOR.** $n_0$ is tuned on HH-X (§3.3, §6 T6), and the ship rule then requires CYM-H to beat the GBM twin on the same HH-X. That is tuning on the decision arena, so the gate is biased toward passing (a PA-0021(a)/PA-0029 holdout violation). Separately, its plan rows bundle generator, model and product work into one row ("Days 1-3"), which breaches A5 despite its claim that each row is one CR.
- **B: MAJOR.** B5 (cross-fitted panel) inherits shared flaw 2: the model-predicted Δη̂ needs pre-2020 legacy features that do not exist. B's otherwise exemplary battery (Holm, calibrated m*, GATE/diagnostic separation) therefore has one gate that cannot be computed as written.
- **C: MAJOR.** X2 has the same pre-cut feature gap, made worse by the AlphaEarth block (starts 2017). The transport back-test threshold τ ≥ 0.80 is uncalibrated: no fair-vs-broken distributions (PA-0021(c)).
- **D: MAJOR.** The decisive T2 kill rule is ">1 bootstrap SE", with no injection-recovery calibration of the primary CC gate on a deliberately broken pipeline (PA-0021(a)). The only injection in D calibrates the control-species margin. The mode heads are also shrunk deviations, so ρ_mode ≥ 0.8 is close to guaranteed (E's point, still open).
- **E: MEDIUM.** The T1 kill is a conjunction. The programme is killed only if CC fails **and** the fall TkL CI includes 0, so either metric alone keeps it alive. The 0.15× and τ ≥ 0.8 back-test thresholds are uncalibrated. P2 bundles generator and product shell. Beyond these, its worst problems are the shared ones.

## Vote
**1st: E (FLUSH-E).** E is the only design whose round-3 method was the project's own PA-0021(a) "can this test fail?" audit. It used that audit to find a real cannot-fail gate shared by B, C and D: shrunk Δ_fall and mode heads compared against their own base. It fixes the problem with an independently fitted fall model whose agreement test is injection-checked over 50 draws. Its stack is the lightest: one-row LightGBM, a 5 GB stencil table, no per-pixel cube. Its phases P0-P5 map onto A5-style CRs with acceptance first (A3). It makes the fewest new-data demands, which reduces exposure to the registration, nodata and year rules this repository has repeatedly broken. Its remaining defects are mostly shared with every design, and all are fixable inside its CR plan.

**5th: A (CYM-H).** The structural idea is defensible, and A's within-site T5 is the only panel test that avoids the pre-2020 feature gap. Through a QMS lens, however, A has the most process exposure. It writes the most new code: a 35-parameter custom model, a support model, a habitat cube for 2016-2026, and lidar via PDAL. Its implementation rows bundle generator, model and product. It tunes $n_0$ on the same HH-X subset its ship rule is decided on. It keeps ">1 SE" kill rules. Its headline bet (HH-X) has unknown power until week 1. It therefore offers the least auditable path to an accepted artifact.

## Elements the winner should adopt
1. **From A:** T5's within-site age curve, fitted directly on clock age-bins with location and year fixed effects. It is the only panel formulation that needs no pre-2020 legacy features. It should replace E's X2 model check, or at least be its primary form.
2. **From B:** Holm-adjusted, fall-decided gate battery with a calibrated cross-play margin m* and stated gate priors. Add these to E's P0 acceptance CR. Use ≥ 50 permutation refits.
