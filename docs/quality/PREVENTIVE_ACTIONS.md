to consult before making a change — not advisory. See `CLAUDE.md` §3 for
how entries are added and superseded. No prose justification here; each
entry links back to its bug investigation for the full story.

Append-only: never delete or water down a rule. A superseded rule stays
listed and says what replaced it.

| PA-0027 | **Supersedes PA-0011** (widens it from retry/polling loops to every handler, and from "log" to "fail closed"). A broad handler (`except Exception`, `except BaseException`, bare `except`, or a tuple containing one) must either re-raise, or resolve to the **fail-closed** outcome of the operation it wraps (refuse, invalid, FAIL, abort, or a result printed/recorded as unknown with the exception type) — never to its success/continue branch: skipping a unit of a batch (a region, a file, a year, a class), returning a value the caller reads as "check passed" or as a designed non-error case ("absent", "optional"), or letting the run exit 0 with outputs that claim completeness.
